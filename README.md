## Projects

### 01 | Hedge Fund Performance Analysis — Research Replication

**Research:** *Do Hedge Funds Hedge?* — Asness, Krail & Liew (2001)

Recreated Exhibit 1 from the published research using Python to analyze historical hedge fund performance and evaluate risk-adjusted returns.

**Key components:**
- Imported and transformed historical monthly investment returns.
- Calculated excess returns, annualized volatility, and Sharpe ratios.
- Evaluated correlations across hedge fund strategies and the S&P 500.
- Replicated published performance statistics and reconciled differences.
- Automated the generation of formatted Excel reporting exhibits.

**Technologies:** Python, pandas, NumPy, Jupyter Notebook, XlsxWriter
